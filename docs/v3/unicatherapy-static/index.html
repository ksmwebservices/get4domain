Our Services

Comprehensive therapy under one roof

Seven services, each designed around the child and family.

  • Occupational Therapy

    Sensory integration, fine motor skills, daily living skills, play and social skills, and school readiness.

  • Physiotherapy

    Gross motor skills, balance and coordination, postural control, mobility training and functional activities.

  • Speech & Language Therapy

    Speech development, language skills, communication, feeding and swallowing, and social communication.

  • Psychological Counselling

    Behaviour and emotional assessment, emotional regulation, parent counselling and learning support.

  • Special Education

    Early intervention, pre-academic skills, academic support and individualised plans for each child.

  • Applied Behaviour Analysis (ABA)

    Behaviour assessment, positive behaviour support, skill building and parent training.

  • ADL & Vocational Skills

    Self-care, independent living, daily routine training, life skills and vocational training.
