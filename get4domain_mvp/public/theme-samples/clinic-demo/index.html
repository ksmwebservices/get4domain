What your first six weeks look like

A genuine sequence, not a sales funnel — this is the order every patient actually moves through.

  1. 1

    Assessment

    45 minutes with a senior physiotherapist: movement history, pain pattern, strength and range-of-motion testing.

  2. 2

    Personalised plan

    A written plan with realistic milestones — what improves first, what takes longer, and why.

  3. 3

    Guided sessions

    Hands-on treatment plus supervised exercise in the clinic, 2–3 times a week depending on the condition.

  4. 4

    Home program

    A short daily routine you can actually keep up, with check-ins to adjust load as you improve.

  5. 5

    Review & discharge

    Re-testing against week one, a maintenance plan, and a clear line for when to come back.

About the clinic

Dr. R. Yogaanand, MPT (Ortho)

Dr. Yogaanand founded this clinic in 2013 after eight years of hospital-based orthopedic and sports rehabilitation work, including time with a state-level athletics team. The clinic still runs on the same principle he trained under: assess properly before you treat, and measure recovery instead of guessing at it.

Today a team of four physiotherapists sees patients across orthopedic, neuro, sports and pediatric care, all following the same intake and progress-tracking process he started with — one file per patient, reviewed every two weeks, not left to memory.

Before you book

No referral is required for most conditions — you can book an assessment directly. For certain post-surgical or neurological cases, we may ask for your surgeon's or physician's notes to plan treatment safely.

We don't manage acute fractures, active infections, or conditions needing emergency care — those go straight to a hospital. If your case needs imaging or a specialist opinion first, we'll tell you plainly during the assessment.

The first assessment runs about 45 minutes. Follow-up sessions are usually 30–40 minutes, depending on whether it's manual therapy, exercise-based, or electrotherapy.

We accept most major insurers for post-surgical rehabilitation with proper documentation. Bring your policy details to the first visit and our front desk will confirm coverage before you start.

Home sessions currently cover T Nagar, Mambalam, Kodambakkam and Ashok Nagar. Let us know your area when booking and we'll confirm availability.

It depends on the condition — a mild sprain might need 4–6 sessions, while post-surgical rehab can run 10–12 weeks. You'll get a realistic estimate in writing after your first assessment, not an open-ended plan.
