Sports Injury Rehab

Ligament tears, sprains and overuse injuries, rebuilt with a return-to-sport plan, not just pain relief.

Book an assessment

What this treats

Getting out of pain and getting back to competing at your previous level are two different goals — and most rehab stops at the first one. We plan sports rehab backwards from the sport itself: what your knee, ankle or shoulder needs to be able to do on the field, and build load, speed and confidence back up to that point in stages.

Conditions we treat

  • ACL and meniscus tears
  • Ankle sprains and instability
  • Hamstring and calf strains
  • Rotator cuff and shoulder instability
  • Tennis, cricket and badminton overuse injuries
  • Return-to-play conditioning after injury

What a session looks like

  1. Initial assessment of the injury plus your sport's specific movement demands
  2. Early-stage pain control and protected range-of-motion work
  3. Progressive strength and agility loading as healing allows
  4. A graded return-to-sport program with clear go/no-go checkpoints
